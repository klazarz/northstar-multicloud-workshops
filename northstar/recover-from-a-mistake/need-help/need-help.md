# Need Help?

## Introduction

If you cannot identify the assigned resources, verify the backup, access SQL, or obtain the expected result, stop at the affected step and contact the facilitator or your environment owner. For a Run on Multicloud reservation, use its supplied support instructions.

### Objectives

* Give support enough evidence to investigate without exposing secrets.

### Prerequisites

* Your current lab and task number and the non-sensitive observations you recorded.

## Task 1: Describe the problem

Share the lab and task, resource names through an approved channel, the operation status, displayed time zone and exact non-sensitive error. Do not share passwords, wallet files, access tokens or private connection details in public messages. Do not retry an uncertain clone submission, restore the source, or change the data to force an expected result. Ask the owner to identify any in-flight clone and its follow-up owner.
